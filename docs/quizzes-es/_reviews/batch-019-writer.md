# Batch 019 (es) - writer notes

Jueces 6-17, 12 files (`judges-quiz-part-6.json` ... `judges-quiz-part-17.json`), all written from
scratch from the rv1909 text printed by `print-chapter`. Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings on all 12.
Verify sheet re-read question by question; the fixes below were made before finishing.

- part-6 (medium) - OK. "Con la langosta" keeps the text's own image ("como langosta", 6:5).
- part-7 (easy) - OK. 7:3 asks how many turned back (veintidós mil); "diez mil" (those who stayed)
  was removed from the distractors so no distractor is a true number for another question.
- part-8 (medium) - Q3 rewritten: the Succoth reply is a rhetorical question, so the answer now
  states what they asked ("Preguntaron si ya tenía en su mano a los dos reyes") instead of
  interpreting it as a refusal.
- part-9 (medium) - Q8 now names Sichêm and cites 9:44-45, because Abimelech also takes Thebes
  later in the chapter (9:50) and "la ciudad" alone was ambiguous.
- part-10 (hard) - list of judges plus the idolatry section; mostly names, places and year counts,
  all written in the verses cited.
- part-11 (medium) - Q4 distractor changed (a question about Jephté's daughter as a wrong answer to
  the embassy question read badly). The vow (11:30-31) and its outcome (11:39-40) are asked only in
  the words the text uses.
- part-12 (hard) - Q5 asks for the word they had to say (Shiboleth); "Siboleth" was deliberately
  kept out of the distractors so the question is not a pronunciation trick.
- part-13 (medium) - OK.
- part-14 (medium) - Q10 reference corrected from 14:19 to 14:20: in rv1909 the Ascalón raid is
  verse 20, verse 19 is the "novilla" reply. Worth noting for other languages' Judges 14.
- part-15 (medium) - OK.
- part-16 (easy, the only non-premium file of the batch) - Q4 now asks about the single occasion
  cited (16:9, after the mimbres), since Dalila repeats the cry in 16:12, 16:14 and 16:20.
- part-17 (medium) - OK. Michâs, teraphim and Beth-lehem de Judá spelled as rv1909 spells them.

Doubts left for the reviewer:
- Jueces 10 and 12 are judge lists; the ten questions are facts (names, tribes, burial places,
  year counts) rather than story beats. That is all those chapters state.
- Jueces 6:5 ("como langosta") and 9:8-15 (the trees' parable) are asked as images, not as claims
  about history, in line with the brief's rule for poetry.
- All `status` values are "draft"; `isPremium` taken from the manifest (only chapter 16 is false).
