Choices worth knowing:

- These chapters are collections of separate sayings, so each question asks what one cited saying
  states (image, contrast, command, who is compared with what). No question asks for a general moral
  the text does not spell out, and none asks about authorship except Proverbios 25:1, where the
  heading itself names Salomón and "los varones de Ezechîas, rey de Judá" (spelled as rv1909 spells it).
- Distractors are taken from other sayings of the same or a nearby chapter but are plainly wrong for
  the cited verse (e.g. in 15:19 "una calzada" belongs to the vereda de los rectos, not to the
  perezoso; in 20:29 "la vejez" is the beauty of the old, not the glory of the young).
- Where a chapter has two near-identical sayings I only used one of them, so no distractor is also
  true elsewhere in the chapter: Proverbios 21:9 (and not 21:19, same mujer rencillosa),
  Proverbios 26:5 (cited with its own stated reason, since 26:4 says the opposite).
- Proverbios 15:3 and 20:10 lost their full quote in the answer option to keep answer lengths even
  (the validator flags a correct answer much longer than the distractors); the full verse wording
  stays in the explanation.
- Correct-answer positions were mixed by hand per quiz (no A-B-C-D rotation), 2-3 per letter,
  never more than two of the same letter in a row.
