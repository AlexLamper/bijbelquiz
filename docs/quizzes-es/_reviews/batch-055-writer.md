Unsure about / worth a reviewer's eye:
- Isaías 12 and 18 are short chapters, so several questions share a verse. No question
  repeats a fact.
- Isaías 13:17 - the correct answer reads "no codiciarán oro ni curarán de la plata",
  following the text's own two clauses rather than paraphrasing them into one.
- Several distractors use images from neighbouring Isaiah chapters (naves de Tarsis,
  setenta años, cordero al enseñoreador). They are plainly wrong for the cited verse,
  but a reviewer may prefer wording further from the book.
