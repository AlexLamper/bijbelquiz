## Doubts / things a reviewer may want to look at

- Éxodo 21:26 (q7, "le dará libertad"): a distractor about losing one's own eye was avoided on purpose,
  because 21:24 ("ojo por ojo") could make it look arguable in this same chapter.
- Éxodo 22:4 ("el duplo") and 22:7 ("el doble") say the same thing in different words; only the 22:4 case is
  asked, and the 22:7-8 question asks what happens when the thief is NOT found, to keep the two apart.
- Éxodo 17:15 (q10, "Jehová-nissi"): distractors are place names from the chapter (Rephidim, Horeb) plus one
  invented altar name; "Massah y Meribah" was kept out because it is the correct answer of q5.
- Éxodo 11 is thin (10 verses); the 10 questions are everything the chapter states, nothing added.
- rv1909 spells the Red Sea "mar Bermejo" and uses "la mar" (feminine); questions keep those words where the
  text uses them.
