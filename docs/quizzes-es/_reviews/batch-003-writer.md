# Batch 003 (es) - writer notes

Genesis 25-36, Reina-Valera 1909, 12 files, 10 questions each. Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings.
Verify sheet re-read for all 120 questions. Names and numbers follow the rv1909 spelling of each
chapter (Rachêl, Sichêm, Issachâr, Nephtalí, Basemat in ch. 26 but Basemath in ch. 36).

- genesis-quiz-part-25 - OK. Q6 uses "cuarenta años" (v. 20); "sesenta años" from v. 26 is kept as a
  distractor because that verse gives Isaac's age at the birth, not at the marriage.
- genesis-quiz-part-26 - OK. Q8 quotes Isaac's own words from v. 22 so that Rehoboth is the only
  possible well; Esek, Sitnah and Seba stay as distractors.
- genesis-quiz-part-27 - OK. Q6 distractors are invented variants of v. 22; real sayings from
  v. 27 and v. 35 were deliberately avoided because they are also true in this chapter.
- genesis-quiz-part-28 - OK.
- genesis-quiz-part-29 - OK. Q6 cites 29:16-17 together, because "hija mayor" comes from v. 16 and
  "ojos tiernos" from v. 17.
- genesis-quiz-part-30 - OK. Q2 counts Jacob's rhetorical question in v. 2 ("¿Soy yo en lugar de
  Dios...?") as him saying he is not in God's place; that is the only reading the verse allows.
- genesis-quiz-part-31 - OK. Q10 asks for the name Jacob gave the heap (Galaad, v. 47); "Mizpa"
  was dropped as a distractor because v. 49 also attaches that name to the place.
- genesis-quiz-part-32 - OK.
- genesis-quiz-part-33 - OK.
- genesis-quiz-part-34 - OK. The chapter's subject is harsh; questions stay on what the verses state
  (the trato, the condition, the killing, Jacob's reproach) without added detail.
- genesis-quiz-part-35 - OK.
- genesis-quiz-part-36 - hard; a genealogy, so most questions are "who is whose son". Q9 originally
  had "duque de los Horeos" as a distractor, which v. 29 makes true of Ana; replaced. Q3 and Q4 both
  cite 36:4 (the verse names two mothers and two sons), which was the only way to get ten distinct
  facts out of the chapter without going into the bare duke and king lists.

Doubts worth a second pair of eyes:
- Chapter 36 is thin on narrative: only v. 24 (Ana and the mulos) and v. 32 (Bela in Dinaba) give
  something other than a name list, so two questions sit on the same verse (36:4).
- In chapter 30 the explanation of Q9 keeps the text's own words "manchadas y de color vario";
  the answer shortens this to "manchadas y las de color oscuro" to stay within the length rule.
