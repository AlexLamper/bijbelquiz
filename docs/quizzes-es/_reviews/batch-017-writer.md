# Batch 017 - writer notes (es, rv1909, Josué 6-17)

12 files written from scratch: `joshua-quiz-part-6` to `joshua-quiz-part-17`.
Validator (`--lang=es --bible-dir=../bijbelapi-data/data`): 0 errors, 0 warnings on all 12.
Verify sheet re-read for all 12; the fixes below were made before this file was written.

## Per quiz

- part-6 (easy, free): the fall of Jericó. Nothing unclear.
- part-7 (medium): Achân. Questions 8 and 9 both cite 7:21 (the confession lists the loot and
  names the hiding place); they ask two different facts. Question 10 was reworded to ask about the
  place where the heap of stones was raised, which is what 7:26 actually states.
- part-8 (medium): Hai and the law read at Ebal. I avoided a question on the number of men sent at
  night, because 8:3 says thirty thousand and 8:12 says "como cinco mil" for the ambush; a reader
  could defend either number.
- part-9 (medium): Gabaón. Question 5 was reworded to "¿qué dejaron de hacer los hombres de
  Israel?" so it asks only what 9:14 says.
- part-10 (easy): the five kings and the sun standing still. Question 6 now names the event
  ("que el sol se detuvo") instead of a vague "esto".
- part-11 (medium): the waters of Merom. The text spells the city both "Hasor" (11:10) and "Asor"
  (11:11, 11:13); each question uses the spelling of the verse it cites, which is why question 5
  says Hasor and question 6 says Asor.
- part-12 (hard): the list of kings. Thin chapter for questions - most of it is a bare list, so
  three questions ask how the list itself names a king or a boundary. Distractor "Desde el Líbano
  hasta el monte de Halac" was replaced, because that is the territory of the kings west of the
  Jordan (12:7) and the question asks about the east side.
- part-13 (hard): land still to be possessed, and the inheritance east of the Jordan. Question 6
  specifies "adivino, hijo de Beor" so that only Balaam fits, since 13:21 also says Moisés struck
  Sehón.
- part-14 (medium): Caleb. Questions 5 and 6 both ask an age (forty when sent, eighty-five that
  day); they are separate facts from 14:7 and 14:10. Distractors in those two questions were
  lengthened so the right answer is not the longest.
- part-15 (hard): the borders and city lists of Judá. Only the narrative parts (13-19) and the
  summary counts carry real questions; the long city lists gave the count in 15:32. Distractor
  "Debir, que antes se llamaba Chîriath-sepher" was replaced, because 15:15 states that as a true
  fact and it could read as arguably correct.
- part-16 (hard): the shortest chapter of the batch, ten verses that are almost all boundary
  description. Ten questions were still written, but seven of them are border points
  (Luz, Beth-oron, Michmetat, Tanath-silo, the Jordán, the arroyo de Cana); nothing richer is in
  the text. Flagging this as the thinnest file of the batch.
- part-17 (medium): Manasés, the daughters of Salphaad and the complaint of the sons of José.
  Question 7 lists six city names, so its distractors were lengthened to keep the length ratio down.

## Doubts left for the reviewer

- part-12 and part-16 are genealogy/boundary chapters; several questions necessarily ask "how does
  the list name X". If that reads as too mechanical, those are the ones to rewrite.
- Spelling is taken verbatim from rv1909 throughout (Achân, Chîriath-arba, Machîr, Lachîs, Jephone,
  Axa, Milchâ, Hai, Asor/Hasor). Please keep these as they are.
- part-11 question 1 names only Jabín rey de Hasor as correct; the other three options are the
  kings this chapter names as recipients of his message, so none of them sent it.
