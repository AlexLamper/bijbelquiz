  "valientes" and "varones de nombre", so the distractors avoid those words.
- part-7 - Génesis 7. q2 ("de siete en siete") and q8 ("quince codos") only use numbers printed
  in the verses. q10 avoids "cuarenta días" as distractor because v17 uses it for the diluvio.

  chapter (Noé does leave the ark and does offer a holocausto, but after v13).

  from v22. q3 avoids "la tierra dará su fruto" as distractor since 8:22 says something close
  (in the previous chapter).
- part-10 - Génesis 10. Nation list; names spelled as rv1909 (Châm, Japhet, Casluim, Joctán).
  q9 uses Gerar and Gaza as distractors - they are places in v19, not sons of Canaán.
- part-11 - Génesis 11. q7 "aquella ciudad" follows v9 ("el nombre de ella Babel"). q10 is the
  stop at Harán, not Canaán, which is where they were heading but did not arrive (v31).
- part-12 - Génesis 12. q10 avoids attributing the ovejas/vacas/camellos of v16 to Jehová;
  the gift came from Faraón, so the distractor was rewritten.
