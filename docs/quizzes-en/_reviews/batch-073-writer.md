on all 12 files. Verify sheet re-read for every question; every marked answer is in the printed verses.

- matthew-bible-quiz-part-15.json - medium. Covers v2-v38. The seven loaves (v34) are not asked; the feeding
  is covered by the four thousand in v38.

Doubts / choices worth a second pair of eyes:
- Jesus is named in questions throughout; every chapter of this batch names him in the text.
- Matthew 9 question 9 has the distractor "Her faith has made you well"-style wording ("Her faith has made
  her well"), which the text says of the woman in v22, not of the girl. The question asks only about the girl,
  so it is plainly wrong there, but a reviewer may want to swap it.
- Matthew 12 question 1 uses "They healed a man's withered hand" as a distractor; the healing in v13 is done
  by Jesus, not the disciples, and is not the Sabbath complaint of v2.
- Matthew 10 question 5 asks only about the money belts (v9), so sandals and staff (v10) are deliberately
  not offered as an answer.
