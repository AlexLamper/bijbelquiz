## Doubts and choices worth a second look

- Luke 11, 12, 15 and 16 never name Jesus in the printed text, so those questions use "he",
  "the speaker" or "the Lord" only. Luke 8, 9, 10, 13, 14, 17, 18 and 19 do name him, so he is
  named there.
- Luke 14:19: the text says "Another said", not "the second", so the question asks about "one of
  the invited men". For the same reason "a field" (14:18, another man's excuse) was not used as a
  distractor, to avoid an arguably-true option.
- Luke 14:22-23: the question is anchored on the servant's report of room left, because "streets
  and lanes" (14:21) is true of the earlier command; the cited verses make only "highways and
  hedges" correct.
- Luke 19:20: the text says "Another came", not "the third servant", so the question says
  "Another servant brought his mina back".
- Luke 13:19 distractors avoid "flour"/"yeast" wording, since 13:21 is a second Kingdom comparison
  in the same chapter and could read as arguably correct.
- Luke 10:17 distractor "wiped the dust off their feet" is an instruction from 10:11, not something
  the seventy reported; kept because the cited verse shows what they actually said.
- Luke 11:11-12: asked as one question over the three pairs (stone/bread, snake/fish,
  scorpion/egg), because asking one pair alone makes the other pair's item look half-right.
- Several correct answers had to be shortened and distractors lengthened to clear the validator's
  length-tell warning; wording stays within the cited verses.
- No chapter in this batch was too thin for 10 questions.
