(12 files, 0 errors, 0 warnings). `categorySlug` "new-testament", `rewardXp` 100, `status` "draft",
`isPremium` from the manifest (false only for chapter 11).

- john-bible-quiz-part-8.json - medium. Question 10 quotes "before Abraham came into existence, I AM" verbatim from verse 58, since the wording is the point of the reaction in verse 59.

- john-bible-quiz-part-14.json - medium. Question 10 asks for the chapter's closing words; the distractors are taken from other chapters so that nothing said earlier in John 14 can also count as correct.
- john-bible-quiz-part-15.json - medium. Question 7 ("what does he no longer call them") uses friends, branches and disciples as distractors: the chapter does call them those, which is exactly why they are wrong for "no longer".
- john-bible-quiz-part-16.json - medium.
- john-bible-quiz-part-17.json - medium. Questions 6 and 7 both come from the prayer about the disciples in the world but cite different verses (11 and 15) and ask different things.
- john-bible-quiz-part-18.json - medium. No question counts Peter's denials: this chapter reports them (verses 17, 25, 27) without naming a number, and the number only appears in chapter 13.
- john-bible-quiz-part-19.json - easy. Question 5 renders the title on the cross in normal capitals; verse 19 prints it in full capitals.

all speakers named in questions (Jesus, Peter, Philip, Thomas, Martha, Mary, Caiaphas, Judas Iscariot,
Pilate, Nicodemus, Barabbas, Malchus, Annas) are named in the chapter they appear in.
