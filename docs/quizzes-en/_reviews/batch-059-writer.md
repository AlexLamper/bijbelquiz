# Batch 059 (en) - writer notes

Jeremiah 20-31, World English Bible (web). 12 files, all `isPremium: true` per manifest,
`status: draft`, 10 questions each. Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings. Verify sheet re-read for all 12.

- part-20: rewritten from scratch (a file already existed). Questions on Pashhur, the stocks,
  Magormissabib, and the prophet's own complaint (v7-15). The speaker in v7-18 is not named in
  the chapter, so those questions say "the speaker".
- part-21: 10 facts from a 14-verse chapter; v5 (Yahweh fighting with outstretched hand) left out
  to keep room for v12-14.
- part-22: "the father" in q8 is the text's own "your father" (v15); the chapter does not name him.
- part-23: q8 distractors deliberately avoid "Peace, peace" and "The message from Yahweh" - both
  are close to what v17 / v33-38 put in the mouths of prophets and people, so they would have been
  arguably true.
- part-24: thin chapter (10 verses). All 10 questions come from those 10 verses, so two questions
  lean on v1 (what was shown; who had been carried captive) and two on v2 (good basket, bad basket).
  Nothing outside the printed text.
- part-25: marked `hard` (long oracle plus the list of nations). Doubt worth a second look:
  q9 asks which king "will drink after all the kingdoms of the world" (v26, Sheshach); the
  distractors (Elam, Tyre, Uz) are kings who also drink the cup earlier in v20-25, but not after
  them all. The question is worded to hinge on "after", so it should be unambiguous - flagging it
  anyway.
- part-26: distractor names kept inside this chapter (Elnathan, Micah, Uriah, Ahikam).
- part-27: q1 follows verse 1 literally ("the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim"), even though
  v3 and v12 speak of Zedekiah; the question says "According to verse 1" for that reason.
- part-28: q6 asks how Jeremiah *first* answered (v6, "Amen! May Yahweh do so"); distractors were
  changed so none of them quotes something Jeremiah says later in v15-16.
- part-29: q9 distractor about Shemaiah removed - he does prophesy a lie (v31), so pairing him with
  Hananiah could read as partly true; the correct answer is the pair of v21.
- part-30, part-31: prophecy questions ask only what the lines say (names, images, quoted sayings);
  no fulfilment or interpretation. Jeremiah 31 q10 cites v31-34 and asks only where the law is put
  (v33).
