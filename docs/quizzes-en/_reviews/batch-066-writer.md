## Doubts / notes

- Distractors sometimes borrow imagery from a neighbouring Daniel chapter (for example a leopard with four
  wings as a wrong answer in Daniel 8). In each case the image does not occur in the chapter being asked
  about, so it is plainly wrong in that text, and no distractor is arguably correct for its question.
- Ezekiel 48 is the thinnest chapter for quiz material (it is largely a list of portions and measurements);
  10 questions were still made, all from what the text states.
- Several correct answers had to be shortened or their distractors lengthened to satisfy the validator's
  answer-length rule; the shortened answers still match the verse (for example "In the plain of Dura" in
  Daniel 3 and "A river too deep to walk through" in Ezekiel 47).
