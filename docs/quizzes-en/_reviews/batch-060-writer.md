- Chapter 37/38 - "the princes" and "the king" are named as the text names them (Zedekiah, Irijah, Ebedmelech all appear in these chapters).
- Chapter 39 - question 1 asks about the breach (verse 2); verse 1's "ninth year, tenth month" is used as a distractor because it dates the start of the siege, not the breach.
- Chapter 39 - question 3 answer shortened to "By night, through the gate between the two walls" for the length rule; verse 4 also mentions the way of the king's garden.
- Chapter 41 - two questions use written numbers (ten men in verse 1, eighty men in verse 5); no counting was inferred.
- Chapter 43 - questions 8-10 name Nebuchadnezzar as the subject of "he" in verses 11-13; the chapter names him in verse 10, where Yahweh says he will send and take him.
- Chapter 43 - question 1 answer omits "all" from "all the proud men" purely for length balance; the other three names in that option list are not in verse 2.
