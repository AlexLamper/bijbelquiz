# Batch 062 - writer notes (en)

Source text: World English Bible (web), printed with `print-chapter.mts`. 12 files, 10 questions each,
status draft, categorySlug old-testament, rewardXp 100, isPremium taken from the manifest
(Ezekiel 1 free, the other 11 premium). Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings.

- lamentations-bible-quiz-part-4 - OK. Verse 3 contrasts the jackals that nurse their young with the
  daughter of my people, who is "cruel, like the ostriches"; the question asks only for the comparison
  the verse makes.
- lamentations-bible-quiz-part-5 - OK. Chapter is one prayer; questions stay with what the lines say
  (inheritance, famine, princes, crown, foxes, the closing request) and claim no author.
- ezekiel-bible-quiz-part-1 - OK. Vision questions ask only images, numbers and what he heard/did.
  Ezekiel is named because 1:3 names him. The four faces here include the ox (1:10); chapter 10 lists
  the cherub instead, so the two quizzes deliberately differ on that point.
- ezekiel-bible-quiz-part-2 - OK. Only 10 verses, so the questions sit close together; question 2 cites
  2:1-3 for the address "Son of man". The speaker is never named beyond "the voice", since the chapter
  does not name him.
- ezekiel-bible-quiz-part-3 - OK. Questions 9 and 10 speak of "the watchman" and "the man" rather than
  naming Ezekiel, because this chapter does not name him. Doubt resolved: an earlier distractor said
  the watchman would be struck mute - that is true elsewhere in the chapter (3:26), so it was replaced.
- ezekiel-bible-quiz-part-4 - OK. All numbers (390 days, 40 days, 20 shekels, sixth part of a hin) are
  written in the verses cited.
- ezekiel-bible-quiz-part-5 - OK. Thirds of the hair (5:2) and thirds of the people (5:12) are two
  separate questions; distractors avoid being partly true about the thirds.
- ezekiel-bible-quiz-part-6 - OK. "Diblah" spelled as the text spells it.
- ezekiel-bible-quiz-part-7 - OK. Chapter is undated oracle language; questions ask what the lines say
  (the end, the rod, buyer and seller, silver in the streets, priest and elders, king and prince) and
  offer no interpretation.
- ezekiel-bible-quiz-part-8 - OK. Names as in the text: Jaazaniah the son of Shaphan, Tammuz.
  The chapter says "about twenty-five men", and the answer keeps "about".
- ezekiel-bible-quiz-part-9 - OK. The man with the inkhorn and the six men are described in the text's
  own words; no identification of who they are.
- ezekiel-bible-quiz-part-10 - OK. Verse 14 lists cherub, man, lion, eagle; the ox of 1:10 is used only
  as a distractor here, which is plainly wrong for this chapter.

Doubts / notes
- Lamentations 4 and 5 and Ezekiel 7 are poetry/oracle without narrative; questions therefore ask about
  images, requests and who is addressed, never about meaning or fulfilment.
- Ezekiel 2 is only 10 verses long, so a few questions draw on the same verse (2:4) from different
  angles; still 10 different facts and 10 different answers.
- No chapter in this batch was too thin for 10 questions.
