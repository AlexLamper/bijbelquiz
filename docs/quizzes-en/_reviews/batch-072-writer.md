# Batch 072 (en) - writer notes

12 files written from scratch (World English Bible text printed by `print-chapter`):
Zechariah 10-14, Malachi 1-4, Matthew 1-3. Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings.
Verify sheet re-read for all 12; every marked answer is in the printed TEXT lines.

## Per quiz

- zechariah-bible-quiz-part-10 - OK, hard. Q6 asks which house Yahweh will *save*; "house of Judah"
  deliberately avoided as a distractor because 10:6 says he will *strengthen* Judah and *save* Joseph.
- zechariah-bible-quiz-part-11 - OK, hard. The chapter names no prophet, so questions say "the speaker"
  (the "I" of 11:7-14). Q2: 11:2 tells both the cypress and the oaks of Bashan to wail, so the question
  pins it to the clause "because the strong forest has come down", which belongs to the oaks.
- zechariah-bible-quiz-part-12 - OK, hard. Q4 (horses struck with blindness): "terror" and "madness"
  avoided as distractors, since 12:4 uses them for every horse and its rider.
- zechariah-bible-quiz-part-13 - OK, hard. Q9 and Q10 both cite 13:9 but ask different facts
  (the refining, and what the people say).
- zechariah-bible-quiz-part-14 - OK, hard. Q2 keeps the text's own "his feet" (14:4); the subject is
  only named in 14:3, which is not cited. Q10: "YAHWEH IS ONE" avoided as a distractor because 14:9
  says Yahweh will be one - replaced with wording that appears nowhere in the chapter.
- malachi-bible-quiz-part-1 - OK, medium. Questions about the offerings say "the priests", whom 1:6
  names as the ones addressed.
- malachi-bible-quiz-part-2 - OK, medium. Q10 condenses 2:17 ("calling the evildoer good in Yahweh's
  sight"); the full quote is in the explanation.
- malachi-bible-quiz-part-3 - OK, medium. Q4's correct answer lists only the first three groups of 3:5
  (sorcerers, adulterers, perjurers); the explanation gives the whole list. Shortened on purpose so the
  correct answer is not the longest option.
- malachi-bible-quiz-part-4 - medium. THIN CHAPTER: only 6 verses, so three questions come from 4:1
  (the furnace, the stubble, neither root nor branch) and two from 4:2. All ten facts are still distinct
  and each is in the cited verse. Q10's answer gives the first half of 4:6; the explanation gives both halves.
- matthew-bible-quiz-part-1 - OK, hard (genealogy), category new-testament. Q9: distractors avoid
  anything else the chapter states about Jesus, so only "save his people from their sins" (1:21) is the
  reason given for the name.
- matthew-bible-quiz-part-2 - OK, easy, new-testament. Q10 cites 2:22-23 because the withdrawal into
  Galilee and the city Nazareth are in the two verses together.
- matthew-bible-quiz-part-3 - OK, easy, new-testament. Q8 and Q10 deliberately avoid using other
  sayings from this same chapter ("Repent, for the Kingdom of Heaven is at hand", "Make his paths
  straight") as distractors, since those are true words in the chapter, just not spoken at that moment.

## Doubts to look at in review

- Zechariah 11 uses "the speaker" throughout; if the reviewer prefers the text's bare "he", the
  wording can be changed without touching the facts.
- Malachi 4 is unavoidably repetitive on verse 1 (see above).
- Several correct answers had to be condensed, and several distractors lengthened, to satisfy the
  validator's answer-length check; wording was kept literal to the printed text where it is a quote.
