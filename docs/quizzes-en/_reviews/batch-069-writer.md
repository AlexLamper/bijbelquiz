## Doubts worth a second pair of eyes

- Micah 1:9 says "it reaches to the gate of my people, even to Jerusalem" in the first person. Q6 was rewritten to ask which city it reaches (answer: Jerusalem) to avoid an answer option in the first person.
- Micah 2:3 "a disaster, from which you will not remove your necks" is rendered in the answer as "a disaster they cannot escape" - a paraphrase, not a quote.
- Micah 2:9 "the women of my people" is rendered "the women of Yahweh's people"; Yahweh is the speaker and is named in 2:3 of the same chapter.
- Amos 6:8 says Yahweh abhors the pride of Jacob and detests his fortresses. Q7 asks only what he abhors, and no distractor mentions fortresses.
- Amos 9:3 mentions both Carmel and the bottom of the sea; Q3 cites the whole verse and asks only about the serpent commanded against those in the sea.
