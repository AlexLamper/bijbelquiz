## Notes and doubts (one line each)

- Jeremiah 44 was rewritten from scratch; the other 11 files are new.
- Jeremiah 45 and 47 are very short; the 10 questions each sit on 10 different
  phrases of the few verses, so several questions cite the same verse (45:1, 47:2).
- Jeremiah 45 q4 asks who is named as speaking in verse 2 - the verse itself says
  "Yahweh, the God of Israel, says to you, Baruch", so the answer is the text's own.
- Jeremiah 48 q2 (Misgab) and q6 (Dibon): several Moabite towns appear twice in the
  chapter; the distractors were chosen so no distractor town is said to be
  "put to shame and broken down" (v1) or told to "come down from your glory" (v18).
- Jeremiah 49 covers five separate oracles (Ammon, Edom, Damascus, Kedar/Hazor,
  Elam); the question stems name which oracle they come from so they are not mixed.
- Jeremiah 51 q6 ("my battle ax and weapons of war") does not name who is addressed,
  because the chapter does not name them.
- Jeremiah 52 q9 asks the total count the chapter itself gives (four thousand six
  hundred); the per-year numbers are not used as distractors to avoid half-true options.
- Lamentations 1-3: no speaker is named anywhere in these chapters, so questions say
  "the speaker", "the city", "he" - no authorship is implied.
- Lamentations 2 q10 cites 2:18-19 because the command in the night runs across both
  verses (the wall is addressed in v18, the command itself is v19).
- Lamentations 3 q6 keeps the text's "we" ("that we are not consumed") as the verse
  has it.
- Prophecy/poetry throughout: questions only ask what the lines say (images, commands,
  who is addressed, place names), never what the prophecy means.
