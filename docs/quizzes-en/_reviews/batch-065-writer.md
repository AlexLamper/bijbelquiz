# Batch 065 (en) - writer notes

Ezekiel 35-46, World English Bible, 12 files x 10 questions, all `draft`, all `isPremium: true`
(from docs/quiz-manifest.json). Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings. Verify sheet re-read for all 12.

- part-35: rewritten from scratch (a file already existed). Q7 paraphrases the boast of v10
  ("These two nations and these two countries will be mine") as "its own possession".
- part-36: medium. Q9 answer is "the stony heart"; "a heart of flesh" and "a new spirit" are in the
  same verse but as what is given, not taken away - reads clearly in the sheet, worth a second look.
- part-37: easy (dry bones). Q4 answer shortened to "A noise and an earthquake" for the length rule;
  the bones coming together is in the explanation.
- part-38: hard. Q7 distractors deliberately lengthened so the correct triple of names is not the
  longest option.
- part-39: hard. Q6 distractors are invented valley names built from names in the chapter
  (Magog, Meshech, Tubal); only "The valley of Hamon Gog" is in the text. Hamonah (v16) is asked
  separately in Q8 as the name of a city, so the two cannot be confused.
- part-40: hard, measurements only. Q1 asks the twenty-fifth year of the captivity; "the fourteenth
  year" from the same verse was avoided as a distractor because that verse also calls it the
  fourteenth year after the city was struck.
- part-41: hard. Distractors for the entrance width (Q2) and the side-room width (Q4) were changed to
  numbers that do not appear in those verses, so no distractor is a different measurement of the
  same verse.
- part-42: hard. Q3 distractor "A wall of five cubits" is not in this chapter (it is in 41:9).
- part-43: medium. Q3 distractors (Jordan, Euphrates, Nile) are river names not in this chapter;
  only the river Chebar is named.
- part-44: hard. Q5 answer limited to "Have oversight at the gates of the house" (v11); the three
  wrong options are things v13 and v17-18 reserve for the priests.
- part-45: hard. Q3 is pinned to "twenty rooms" (v5) so that the Levites' portion cannot be confused
  with the priests' portion of the same size in v3-4.
- part-46: hard. Q5 (Sabbath) and Q6 (new moon) are deliberately adjacent; the answers differ by the
  young bull, exactly as v4 and v6 read.

Doubts / for the reviewer:
- Ezekiel 40-46 are measurement chapters; several questions are plain numbers and read dryly. All
  are literally in the cited verse, but some chapters offered little else.
- Speakers are never named beyond what the chapters themselves say: Yahweh / the Lord Yahweh, "the
  son of man", "the prince", "the man" with the measuring reed. Ezekiel's own name is not used in
  any question, since these chapters do not name him; questions say "the prophet" or "he".
- Ezekiel 42 and 46 both mention boiling places; "the boiling houses" is only used as a distractor
  in 42 and 43, never as an answer outside Ezekiel 46 (where it is not asked).
