Doubts / choices worth a second look by the reviewer:
- Distractors often borrow images from neighbouring Revelation chapters (sickle, censer, bowls, Wormwood, Abaddon). They are plainly wrong in the cited chapter, but a reviewer should confirm none is also true in the chapter where it is used.
- Revelation 6:8 carries two questions (the name "Death" and "one fourth of the earth"); Revelation 13:2 likewise (the three animals and what the dragon gave him). Different facts, same verse.
- Difficulty: 9, 11 and 17 set to hard (long symbolic descriptions, the angel's explanation), the other nine to medium.
